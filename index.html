Now Under the Arches

Seven months, a few false starts, and one very stubborn search for the right building later, Edges found its permanent home in a railway arch on Bridge Road, Holbeck. Every pie we make is no less than 18 inches, by the slice or whole, pre-ordered online and ready for collection. We pride ourselves on perfection — and we're not settling for anything less.

Three Trips to New York

Edges Pizza started with Grace Ward and her partner Edge, two Harrogate locals who kept coming home from New York City craving one thing: a proper slice. Thin, crispy, and folded in half — the kind of pizza Leeds didn't have. So they built it themselves, running pop-ups across the north and finding a home for a summer on the rooftop terrace at Green Room.
